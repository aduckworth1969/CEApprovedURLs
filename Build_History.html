How to read this
  • 11 of 12 builds are marked reconstructed. Their counts were read back from pages those builds generated — committed copies and local backups — rather than measured while the build ran. The numbers are the builds' own; only the manner of recording differs.
  • The page's scope changed on 16 Sep 2026, when it began building only rows DOC tags IncarEducation. Earlier builds covered the whole approved whitelist, so the drop from 137 to 103 sites is a narrowing of scope, not sites being withdrawn. The line is broken there for that reason, and totals either side are not comparable.
  • Some earlier builds published the same site twice under two spellings of its address. Where that happened the run table shows a lower “distinct” count beside the published total.
  • Link checks run from a staff network, not from inside a facility. A site reachable here can still be blocked by the facility filter, and an internal-only host can fail here yet work there. Blocked to automation is counted separately from broken for that reason — those hosts answered, they just refuse automated requests.
